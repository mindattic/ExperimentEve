# EXPERIMENT EVE — World Gazetteer

Design reference: the real Newport, RI area. **No real name appears in-game** —
every place gets a nod/wink name (influences visible to anyone who knows the
area), no state, no country. Everything is re-imagined as it was on
**Sunday, June 21, 1998**. Modern-only businesses are back-cast to 1998
equivalents; what has no 1998 equivalent is cut (a "FOR LEASE" sign is very 1998).

**The keystone wink**: the region's most famous writer's gravestone reads
"I AM PROVIDENCE." So the city is **Kingsport** — his own invented coastal
town — and the destination stays **Providence**, which now winks twice
(the city up the bay, the word for paternal care, and the epitaph).

## Districts & gameplay hooks

- **the Cliff Walk** — linear coastal corridor between the Summer Houses and
  open sea; fog, Sting Gulls over the rail, Shellbacks on the rocks; one-way
  drops to the shore break chases. Fixed cameras were invented for this.
- **Beauview Avenue / The Swells** — the bauble economy's endgame: pearls,
  silverware, pocket watches by the drawer; dust sheets like ghosts; Erasure
  evacuated the Avenue FIRST (the placards downtown noticed).
- **Stella Maris College** — dorms inside gilded guest wings; summer session
  meant somebody was home.
- **the Casino** — floodlit grass courts; "HALL OF FAME OPEN — JULY" banners
  for a July that won't come; 13 courts, 13 camera zones; exhibition-match
  arena beat.
- **the Driftwood Library** — the public-internet terminal with a printed
  directory of websites; a special ApertureOS machine; a librarian survivor
  who files everything, including what happened.
- **Fort Addams** — the folk-festival grounds hold an abandoned stage
  ("JUNE 20 BENEFIT — CANCELLED"); survivor stronghold vs. a Bulwark Moose
  that keeps breaching the same wall; festival rigging = salvage heaven.
- **the Drive** — bicycle showcase: ten miles of coast road, pack chases,
  dead cars with trunk tools; ends at the Point Light and the Inn on the
  Point (sunset chairs still aimed at the view; guest book stops mid-signature).
- **First Beach** — snack bar, arcade with a *Mega 64* demo kiosk, Shellbacks
  owning the sand, a carousel that runs all night on its own.
- **Second Beach** — dunes and a Y2K prepper camp in the parking lot; prepped
  for the wrong apocalypse, eighteen months early.
- **the Wharfs** — boats leaving for the Island all night, returning empty;
  the pawnbroker's supplier; Mo's Clam Shack (fryer still warm); the Lobster
  Co-op (a Trashclaw nest, of course); Two Scoops ("FATHER'S DAY SUNDAE —
  DADS EAT FREE" still on the board).
- **Hotel Norseman** — rooftop view of half the map; room keys are actual
  keys: floor-by-floor exploration gating with zero padlock puzzles.
- **Billy Island** — the Harbor Light save point (nearest to the slice) and
  the Lodge: the Erasure checkpoint civilians were told was a shelter.
- **the *Providence Belle*** — 1928 motor yacht; the endgame vehicle up the
  Bay once the Bell Bridge fails. Fueling and crewing her is the late arc.
- **the Out Island** — sequel bait: the place people swear the Experiment
  never reached; a ferry schedule with every departure crossed out but one.
- **Kingsport → Midway → Northgate** — the Quiet Isle's three towns are the
  campaign's three acts, north to the bridges, then up the Bay to Providence.

## Standing rules (from the main plan)
- Nod-names only; influences visible, real names never. No state, no country.
- 1998 only; back-cast modern things; cut what can't back-cast.
- Lighthouses save (ApertureOS 98 machines); nests spawn; factions war;
  Father's Day is the through-line; the Island (the old Asylum) is where the
  city has sent what it can't explain since 1819.
